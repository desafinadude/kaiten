# Kaiten print files

Everything here is generated by `node tools/make_print.cjs` from the game data in `index.html`. Re-run it after a rules or art change.

## Print settings

- **Scale:** print at **100% / actual size**. Turn off "fit to page", or the cards and the cut marks will not line up.
- **Sides:** print the card and token sheets **double-sided, flipping on the long edge**. Every front page is followed by its back, already mirrored to line up.
- **Paper:** card stock, 250–300 gsm. For tokens, use thicker board or glue the sheets onto greyboard before cutting.
- **Colour:** the files are RGB. A print shop will convert them, so ask for a proof if colour matters.

You need either the A3 boards or the 4×A4 boards, not both. For the travel set, print the kitchen cards instead of the boards and lay the belt out as a row of dish cards.

**Cards** are 63 × 88 mm (poker size), nine to an A4 sheet. They sit edge to edge, so a single straight cut separates two cards. A 3 mm bleed runs around the outside of each block. Cut on the crop marks in the margins: two cuts across and two down for each row and column.

**Tokens** have a 1.6 mm coloured halo outside the dashed cut circle, so a slightly wide cut still looks clean. A 22 mm circle punch or a craft cutter is easiest.
